Origin Story

Every support engineer has one. Here's how this one got started.

Boban spent two years as an L2 Application Support Engineer, deployed at TVS Motor Company through Macs InfoSolutions, keeping two critical platforms running: the Enquiry Management System (EMS) for two- and three-wheeler sales, and the Dealer Management System (DMS) for service and parts.

The job spans dealer networks across India and international markets in Bangladesh, Spain, Latin America, and Nigeria — troubleshooting live issues, running UAT before releases, and translating dealer feedback into fixes the development team could act on.

Comfortable in AWS (EC2, S3, EBS, IAM, VDI) and SQL-based data validation, with a track record of keeping systems available through the highest-pressure stretch of the year: festival and peak sales season.
